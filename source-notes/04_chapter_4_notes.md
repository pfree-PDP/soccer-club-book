# Chapter 4

Building the Environment

---

## Central Idea

The player is at the center.

Every adult exists to help the player improve themselves.

Adults cannot develop players.

Adults create conditions where players develop themselves.

---

## Coach

Owns:

Training

Lineups

Development

Culture

Relationships

Vision

Coach is the CEO of the team.

Delegate responsibilities.

Never delegate relationships.

---

## Team Manager

Owns:

Communication

Logistics

Travel

Player cards

Ref fees

Equipment

Readiness

Simple version:

Coach handles the soccer.

Manager handles the stuff.

---

## Assistant Coach

Purpose:

Complement the coach.

Strengthen weaknesses.

Challenge privately.

Support publicly.

One voice on the field.

---

## Parents

Operate at the player level.

Responsibilities:

Rest

Nutrition

Attendance

Transportation

Encouragement

Model behavior

Support development

---

## Volunteers

Support the environment.

Every role should make player development easier.

---

## Scaling

Responsibilities stay largely the same.

Titles become more specialized.

Youth Team

↓

Club

↓

Professional Academy

Same responsibilities.

Different organization.

---

## Chapter Closing

Remember why you said yes.

Everyone should make the environment better.

Not harder.

Bridge:

When someone begins making the environment harder...

Chapter 5 begins.
