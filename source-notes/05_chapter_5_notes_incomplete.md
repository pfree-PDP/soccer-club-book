# Chapter 5

Protecting the Environment

Working Theme:

Sometimes leadership means protecting the environment rather than keeping everyone happy.

---

## Opening Story

Parent fundamentally misaligned.

Believed:

- their player deserved more minutes
- coach should coach differently
- roster should change
- other players shouldn't play
- team should exist differently

Problem wasn't disagreement.

Problem was misalignment.

---

## Core Principle

Every role exists to make player development easier.

When someone's behavior makes development harder, leadership requires action.

---

## Themes

Hard conversations

Role clarity

Boundaries

Volunteer fit

Parent agendas

Protecting culture

Consistency

---

## Transition

Building the environment is leadership.

Protecting the environment is leadership too.
