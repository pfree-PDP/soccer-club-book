# Chapter 1 Notes

## Opening Story

Driving kids to soccer practice.

Kids were trying several sports.

Someone asked:

"Would you referee?"

It seemed like a small volunteer role.

It wasn't.

---

## First Lessons

Had to:

- Attend classes
- Learn Laws of the Game
- Become certified
- Referee games

Experienced:

- fear
- responsibility
- imposter syndrome

---

## Memorable Story

Late-game offside decision.

Disallowed goal.

Coach questioned decision.

Assistant referee wasn't certain.

Realized:

Officials make mistakes.

Responsibility feels heavy.

---

## Lessons Learned

Refereeing forced me to truly learn soccer.

It created empathy for referees.

It taught me:

- criticism comes with leadership
- showing up matters
- learning requires discomfort

---

## Learning Loop

Introduce once.

Do NOT overuse.

Pattern:

Say Yes

↓

Overwhelmed

↓

Learn

↓

Competent

↓

Mastery

↓

Say Yes Again

Recurring background rhythm throughout the book.

---

## Chapter Ending

Every journey begins differently.

Referee.

Coach.

Assistant Coach.

Team Parent.

Volunteer.

But eventually every path leads to...

The Team.
