# You Said Yes
## A Practical Handbook for Parents, Coaches and Volunteers Who Suddenly Find Themselves Running a Soccer Team or Club

## Core Premise

Most youth soccer organizations are built by parents who simply said "yes."

This book is both a story and a handbook.

It follows one parent's journey from driving his son to practice, to becoming a referee, coach, club leader, and eventually studying inside a professional European academy.

Each chapter combines:

- Personal story
- Lessons learned
- Practical "how-to" guidance
- Reflection
- Resources for deeper study

The reader should always feel like they are learning alongside the author.
